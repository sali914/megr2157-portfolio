## Defending an Engineering Decision

To me, defending an engineering decision simply means proving why a design choice is the right one using hard data, core physics principles, test results, and clear trade-off evaluations, rather than just going off a hunch or personal preference. Right now, as a 3rd year, I have an idea of what it takes to back up a design decision—especially through setting parameters and tolerances in software like SolidWorks, but I am still building up the hands-on experience to do it seamlessly in practice. Defending a design takes accurate calculations and clear models, but it also takes the communication skills to take constructive feedback from teammates and advisors, which is something I am actively sharpening through my coursework and team projects.
